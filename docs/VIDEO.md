# Demo Video Script — Hidden Problems in Tickets

> **Codegeist 2026** · Category: **Apps for Business Teams**  
> Duration: **under 5 minutes** (target 4:45) · Reference: **§20 of the specification**  
> Spoken language: **English**

This document lays out the exact minute-by-minute plan for the submission video. It follows the narrative arc of §20, shows the real figures from the sample dataset (43 tickets, 61 hours, 38 people), and lists fallback plans for every recording step.

---

## 1. Overview & Dataset Anchor

- **Dataset**: 120 tickets across 60 days, pre-loaded into Forge SQL (§20).
- **Core story**: three hidden problems lurk in the queue, invisible to individual agents. We reveal them, confirm the largest one, recognize incoming tickets live, alert customers on the portal, show the bill to developers, handle the fix with regression watch, and query the Rovo agent and MCP tool.
- **Reference figures** (from the specification walkthroughs §7.1–§7.4; the sample set displays its own exact counts once loaded):
  - VPN disconnects after a workstation update: **43 tickets** in 3 weeks, **61 support hours**, **38 people touched**, rising (§7.1). After removing 2 false positives and confirming: **41 tickets linked** to problem issue **PRB-12**; at fix time, **17 open tickets and 23 "me too"** (§7.4).
  - A floor printer and duplicate invoices: the two other hidden problems of the sample set (§20) — exact counts shown by the Problems page at recording time.
  - Password resets: a costly recurring request classified apart as an automation candidate (§18).

## 3. Fallback Plans During Recording

1. **Pre-record clean B-roll**: Before recording voiceover, record 5 minutes of clean 4K screencast navigating through all 7 screens with the sample dataset pre-loaded.
2. **Deterministic seed**: The sample dataset is generated from a fixed seed (`src/sample/dataset.ts`, contract WP-O in [docs/CONTRATS.md](CONTRATS.md), being merged); if the state gets messy during practice, one click on "Remove sample set" followed by "Load sample set" restores the exact numbers in seconds.
3. **No network dependency**: Everything runs inside Forge SQL; zero external API downtime risk.
4. **Subtitles**: Prepare burned-in English subtitles for judges watching with audio muted.
